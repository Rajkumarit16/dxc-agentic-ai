# Connecting to VPN

**Category:** Network  
**ID:** KB-004

Use **OrbitConnect VPN** for all remote access to internal systems.
1. Open OrbitConnect, choose the nearest gateway (Chennai, Bengaluru, Frankfurt, Dallas).
2. Sign in with your Orbit account and approve MFA.
Error 809 or "gateway timeout": switch gateway, then restart the OrbitConnect service. Hotel and airport Wi-Fi may block VPN — use the "TCP 443" mode in settings.
VPN disconnects every 12 hours by design; reconnect to continue.
