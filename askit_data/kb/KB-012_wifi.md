# Wi-Fi in Offices

**Category:** Network  
**ID:** KB-012

**Orbit-Corp** Wi-Fi is for Orbit laptops (automatic sign-in by certificate).
**Orbit-Guest** is for visitors and personal phones: daily password at reception, internet only.
Laptop not connecting to Orbit-Corp: forget the network, restart, and make sure the laptop was on VPN in the last 30 days (certificate renewal).
