# Requesting a New Laptop

**Category:** Hardware  
**ID:** KB-006

New joiners get a laptop on Day 1 if HR submitted the joining form 5 working days earlier.
Standard models: Developer (32 GB RAM) and Business (16 GB RAM). Developer laptops need manager approval.
Raise a **Hardware Request** in the portal. Delivery: 3 working days in metro offices, 5 elsewhere.
