Orbit Corp travel expenses. Every travel expense claim must be filed in the Orbit Spend portal with receipts. Each travel expense claim needs manager approval before Finance reviews it, and an incomplete claim is returned to the employee. Approved claims are paid on the fifth of the next month.

Late filing rule. The reimbursement window closes 21 days after you return from the trip. After that window, a trip needs written approval from the Finance Controller before any money is paid back. Keep the boarding passes and hotel invoices until the payment reaches your bank account. Thank you.

Orbit Corp cafeteria timings. The cafeteria opens at 8 AM on weekdays and serves lunch until 2 PM. On Fridays the cafeteria closes early at 3:30 PM, because the kitchen team prepares for the weekend. Tea and coffee machines on every floor stay available until the office closes at 7 PM. Thank you.

Laptop refresh. Employees receive a new laptop every four years, and the old device is wiped by the IT team before it leaves the building. Request a replacement earlier only if the laptop fails the hardware health check, and bring the charger and the carry case along. This applies to all staff.

Visitor badges. Every visitor must be registered by the host employee before arrival, and the reception desk prints a temporary badge on the day. Visitors wear the badge at all times, are escorted in meeting areas, and return the badge at the gate when they leave the campus. Please follow this.

Printer help. Shared printers are named after the floor they sit on, and the toner is replaced by the facilities team on Tuesday mornings. If a print job is stuck, cancel it from the queue, restart the print spooler on your laptop, and send the job again to the nearest printer. Please follow this.

Team offsites. Each team may plan one offsite per quarter, and the manager books the venue through the travel desk. Offsites are meant for planning and bonding, so keep the agenda light, share the plan with HR two weeks ahead, and collect feedback from the team afterwards. Please read it once.

Cafeteria menu this month. The cafeteria serves South Indian breakfast on Mondays, a North Indian thali on Wednesdays, and a Friday biryani special. Vegetarian, vegan and jain options are marked on the cafeteria menu board near the cafeteria entrance every morning for easy choice. Keep it simple.

Cafeteria payments and feedback. Pay at the cafeteria with the Orbit badge wallet or a Friday meal coupon. Cafeteria feedback goes to the facilities desk, and the cafeteria vendor reviews the Friday menu with the facilities team every month, so please keep your comments coming in. Keep it simple.

Cafeteria hygiene. The cafeteria vendor is audited every quarter for food safety. Friday cafeteria staff rotate to the weekend kitchen, and the cafeteria seating area is cleaned after every lunch service, with tables sanitised and the floor mopped before the next group arrives for lunch. Thanks.

Datacenter safety rules. Only authorised engineers may enter the Chennai datacenter, and every visitor signs the access register at the gate. Never restart or unplug a rack without a change ticket and a second engineer. For a datacenter power failure, call the facilities duty manager on extension 4471. Report the rack number and the time of failure, then raise a Critical ticket in the service portal so the incident team can follow up.
